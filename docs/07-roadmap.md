Each phase ends in a state a reviewer can verify. No phase starts until the previous exit gate passes. Durations assume a small team (1–3 engineers) and are estimates, not commitments.

## Why this order

- **P1 before backend:** The fastest, cheapest way to perfect the entire student experience—including the 14-step conversational AI onboarding interview, dynamic Vault, Gemini Canvas, and mobile/desktop responsiveness—while locking the exact TypeScript domain contract (`src/domain/`).
- **P2 before P3:** The Catalogue, Portal Blueprint inspector, and University Semester Cache contain zero personal data, letting us validate discovery and portal mapping safely.
- **P3–P5:** Connects the student's Vision-extracted Vault, Evidence Ledger, Gmail autonomous OTP pickup, and isolated cloud browser execution into a seamless "tap `Declare & Submit` and sleep" pipeline.
